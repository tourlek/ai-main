# Leadership update

1. Preserve the source's outcome, customer/business impact, evidence, uncertainty, owner, and next decision/action.
2. Replace implementation details with their practical consequence; retain product names, issue IDs, numbers, and dates needed for tracking.
3. Match the requested channel. If unspecified, provide a concise paste-ready update rather than blocking on a formatting choice; ask only when audience or factual ambiguity changes the meaning.
4. Distinguish proposed, implemented, validated, and released work. Do not invent impact, deadlines, ownership, or successful validation.
5. Return a draft; writing for a channel does not authorize posting to it or modifying the engineering source record.
